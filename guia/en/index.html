In short

This is a free kit for the owner of a small or mid-sized business to move past casual ChatGPT use and put an AI agent to work on a real process. 📖 Words in this guide

  • Agent: an AI that doesn't just chat, it does the task (reads the message, checks the calendar, replies, logs it). ChatGPT answers; the agent works.
  • Token: a piece of text the AI reads or writes (about 4 letters). AI is priced per million tokens.
  • API: the door through which one system talks to another; the agent uses the AI through it and pays per use.
  • Isolated environment: an account or machine just for the agent, where it can't reach the rest of the company.
  • Password vault: a protected place for passwords and keys; the agent uses them without ever seeing the password text.
  • OWASP: an open foundation that publishes the most widely used security risk lists in the world.
  • CSV: a plain-text spreadsheet that opens in Excel and Google Sheets.

User guide · the tools

Three tools, in this order

1

Process X-ray: where to start

List the tasks that repeat. For each one, say how many times a week it happens, how many minutes it takes, whether the rule is clear, whether the information is already on the computer and how much damage a mistake would do. How the score works: hours per month × rule clarity × data availability ÷ risk. It is a ruler for ranking, not the truth. A high-risk task can go in the queue, but only with a person approving every answer.

2

Calculator: what it costs and what it pays back

Take the first one in the queue. Compare today's cost (people doing it) with the agent's cost (the AI bill plus someone's time reviewing). The metric that matters is cost per task, counting the review and the tasks that go back to a person.

3

Safe agent checklist: can you switch it on?

The agent works on its own and touches files, accounts and messages. The rule from the keynote is least privilege: it only accesses what the task needs, and someone keeps an eye on it. Tick what is already guaranteed.

Items 2, 4 and 10 are required: without any of them the result stays at supervised pilot.

Based on the principle of least privilege and on the risks in the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications (2026), translated for non-technical readers. The text version is in kit/checklist-agente-seguro.md (in Portuguese).

4

30-day pilot and measurement

Build the agent for the first process with the kit the X-ray recommends. Over the 30 days, write down every week: how many tasks it did, how many went back to a person, how much the AI bill was and how many complaints there were. At the end, redo the calculator with those numbers. If the difference is positive and the checklist is still green, move on to the second in line.
