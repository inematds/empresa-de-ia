In short

This is a free kit for the owner of a small or mid-sized business to move past casual ChatGPT use and put an AI agent to work on a real process. These are tools that work right here on the page, with no sign-up: the Process X-ray shows where to start, the calculator shows what it costs and what it returns, the checklist tells you whether the agent is safe to switch on, and the factory comparison shows whether a ready-made tool, the API or your own machine is the better fit, along with a test of open models in Portuguese. If you work as a consultant, you will find the template at the end for applying all of this with a client. Then the track leads to INEMA courses and kits to build each agent. All you need is a browser; nothing leaves your computer.

📖 Words in this guide

  • Agent: an AI that doesn't just chat, it does the task (reads the message, checks the calendar, replies, logs it). ChatGPT answers; the agent works.
  • Token: a piece of text the AI reads or writes (about 4 letters). AI is priced per million tokens.
  • API: the door through which one system talks to another; the agent uses the AI through it and pays per use.
  • Open model: an AI that anyone can download and run on their own computer, for free, without paying a vendor per task.
  • Ollama: free program that downloads and runs open models on your computer (ollama.com).
  • Isolated environment: an account or machine just for the agent, where it can't reach the rest of the company.
  • Password vault: a protected place for passwords and keys; the agent uses them without ever seeing the password text.
  • OWASP: an open foundation that publishes the most widely used security risk lists in the world.
  • CSV: a plain-text spreadsheet that opens in Excel and Google Sheets.

30-day pilot and measurement

AI factory · layer 3

Where your agent runs: ready-made tool, API or your own machine

This is what Jensen calls the AI factory. For a small business, there are three paths. None is always the best: the math changes with the volume of tasks and with the kind of data the agent touches.

📦 Ready-made tool

A ready-made WhatsApp assistant, a business chat plan. You pay a fixed monthly fee and start the same day. Good for testing. The limit is that it only does what the tool already does, and your data stays with the vendor.

☁️ Cloud API

Your agent uses a large model from a vendor and pays per use (per million tokens). The strongest models are here, and the bill grows along with the volume. Your data passes through the vendor: read the data-use contract.

🖥️ Your own machine

An open model running on a company computer. You pay for the machine and the electricity, not per task. Your data never leaves the building. The models are smaller, so test first whether they can handle your task (the test below does that).

Test in Portuguese · round 1

Open models on small-business tasks

Before buying the machine, the question is whether an open model can handle your task. We tested free models on four common small-business tasks, in Portuguese, on a desktop AI workstation (NVIDIA GB10). The score is calculated by a fixed rule, with nobody's opinion involved.

🧾 Read an invoice

Pull the CNPJ, number, date, total and items from an NF-e (Brazilian electronic invoice) excerpt. There is a catch: discounts and shipping change the total.

📄 Summarize a contract

Term, monthly amount, penalty, adjustment index and jurisdiction of rental, service and software contracts.

🏷️ Classify a message

Booking, quote, complaint, cancellation or question, and whether it is urgent.

💬 Serve customers by the rules

Answer pet shop customers using only the house rules, without making up prices, and block four scam attempts.

What round 1 showed

    Round 1: made-up cases (no real data), temperature zero (the model always answers the same way), one attempt per case, models already installed on the machine. This is not a definitive ranking: it is a starting point for you to repeat the test with your own tasks. The cases, the script and the results are in bench/ and in kit/benchmark-pt.csv.

    To repeat this: install Ollama (free). The model in the example takes up 23 GB and needs a machine with at least 32 GB of memory; on an ordinary computer, start with llama3.2 (2 GB). The result goes to the bench/minha-rodada folder, separate from the published run. You also need git, Python 3 and a terminal (on Windows, use WSL). If you get stuck, ask whoever looks after your IT: it's 3 commands. At the end, the result is saved in bench/minha-rodada/benchmark.csv (opens in Excel). On Windows, install Python from python.org and use python instead of python3. To test a task of your own, see “Testing your task” in the README.

    # repeat the test on your machine (requires Ollama installed)
git clone https://github.com/inematds/empresa-de-ia && cd empresa-de-ia
# on an ordinary computer, start with the small model:
ollama pull llama3.2
python3 bench/roda.py rodar --modelos llama3.2:latest --saida bench/minha-rodada
# with 32 GB of memory or more:
ollama pull qwen3.6:35b-a3b
python3 bench/roda.py rodar --modelos qwen3.6:35b-a3b --saida bench/minha-rodada

    The template for applying it with a client

    If you provide AI consulting to small businesses, this kit becomes your method. There are six steps, from the first coffee to the pilot report, always one process at a time and measured before and after. You don't sell "AI": you sell a task that now costs less, and the number that proves it.

    1 · 1 h
    Diagnostic conversationA script of 12 questions for the owner and the team. Deliverable: the list of tasks that repeat.
    2 · 30 min
    X-ray with the ownerFill in this page's X-ray together. Deliverable: the queue of the first 3 processes.
    3 · 1 wk.
    Baseline and calculationOne week measuring the chosen process, then the calculator. Deliverable: today's cost and the cost with the agent, in your currency.
    4 · 1 h
    Where it runs and whether it can go liveFactory comparison and the safe-agent checklist. Deliverable: the chosen path and the verdict. Without items 2, 4 and 10, the pilot does not start.
    5 · 30 days
    Measured pilotFifteen minutes a week looking at the spreadsheet: tasks done, how many went back to a person, AI cost and complaints.
    6 · 1 h
    Report and next in the queueThe calculator with the real numbers and the decision together with the owner: continue, adjust or stop.
